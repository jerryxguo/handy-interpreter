Handy Interpreter Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 November 2026

Summary

Handy Interpreter translates spoken conversations, talks and announcements. It has no accounts, advertising or analytics, and doesn't save recordings or transcripts on your device. When AI mode is allowed and turned on, some recordings and text are sent to third-party AI services to recognize and translate them, as described below.

Microphone

The app listens while it's running and not paused. In Talk mode it turns the microphone off while it translates and speaks. In Listen mode it keeps listening throughout, so nothing is missed. Recorded audio is kept in the iPhone's memory only until the phrase has been processed. It is not saved.

Speech recognition

Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognizer. This happens on your iPhone when the language supports it. Otherwise Apple may process the audio on its servers, under Apple's privacy policy.

AI mode

When AI mode is on, a recording of what was said is sent over an encrypted connection to the app's server on Amazon Web Services in Singapore if the speech was, or may have been, Chinese, or if the iPhone's own speech recognizer couldn't make out any words in it. In a conversation with Chinese, that's whenever the iPhone doesn't recognize the speech as the other language. The server forwards them to Alibaba Cloud's Qwen speech recognition service in Singapore and returns the text to your iPhone.

The app's server does not store the audio or the text. Like any online service, it receives your IP address with each request, but it doesn't log it. Its logs contain only processing times, data sizes and error messages, and they are deleted after 14 days. In Listen mode, the text of what was said, with up to three passages before it for context, is also sent to the app's server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to be translated and returns the translation to your iPhone.

Alibaba Cloud handles the audio, and Google the text, under their own terms, and each provider may keep technical records as part of running its service.

With AI mode off, nothing is sent to this server.

AI mode preview

During the complimentary preview, AI mode requires no purchase, and the app sends nothing to the app's server that identifies you or your Apple Account, apart from the IP address every connection carries. Every copy of the app presents the same preview key to the server, which counts total daily requests across all preview users and deletes daily counts after two days.

Translation and voice

Translation uses Apple's Translation framework with languages downloaded from Apple. In Listen mode with AI mode on, Google's Gemini translates instead, as described under AI mode, and Apple's translation is used when Gemini is slow or unavailable. Translations are spoken by your iPhone's built-in voices.

What is stored on your iPhone

The app stores your language choices and settings, and a log of how long each step took. The log contains timings and language names, never speech or text, and it is replaced each time the app opens. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or used for advertising. It is shared only with the service providers described above, to provide the app's features.

Other people

The app hears everyone nearby. Please tell people in the conversation that it is listening. In Listen mode, the person speaking may not know the app is listening, so make sure recording and translating them is allowed where you are.

Children

Handy Interpreter is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.
